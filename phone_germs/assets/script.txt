Your phone is probably dirtier than a toilet seat. You'd think the toilet is the dirtiest thing you touch all day. It's literally where waste goes, but toilet seats get scrubbed with disinfectant almost every day. Your phone almost never does. So every door handle, every dollar bill, and every snack you touch ends up right on your screen. And your phone stays warm in your pocket, which can help some germs survive longer. Then you carry it into the bathroom, where tiny droplets from flushing can float through the air and land on it. And then you press that same screen right against your face. So the dirtiest thing in your bathroom might be the one in your hand. And a quick wipe once a day removes most of them.
