## Description
At 12:51 PM on 22 February 2011, the ground beneath Christchurch, New Zealand moved upward faster
than gravity. Five months earlier, a far bigger magnitude 7.1 earthquake had struck without killing
anyone directly. So why did a smaller magnitude 6.2 quake take 185 lives?

This documentary follows the September 2010 Darfield earthquake, the hidden fault under the Port Hills,
liquefaction in the eastern suburbs, the collapse of the CTV and PGC buildings, the rescue, and the
city that rebuilt itself.

Visuals are CG reconstructions and illustrative graphics, not archival footage.
