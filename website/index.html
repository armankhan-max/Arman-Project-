Questions

Before you ask…

Is this a fashion book?

No. There are no trends and nothing that requires you to be young, slim or wealthy. It's a practical fitting-room manual: what fits, what to alter and what to buy.

Do I need to buy expensive clothes?

Not at all. The whole idea is that fit beats price. Most of the biggest improvements come from small, routine alterations and better choices — not bigger budgets.

I'm not good with computers. How do I get it?

After you buy, you'll receive an email with a download link. Click it and the guide opens on your computer, tablet or phone. You can also print it — many pages are designed to be printed.

Is the print large enough to read comfortably?

Yes. The guide uses large, clear type, short sections and plenty of tables and checklists, so it's easy to read on a tablet or printed out.

I'm buying it for my father or husband. Is that okay?

Absolutely — Chapter 10 is written especially for family members, with a size and style sheet and advice on helping without hurting his pride.

Does it name brands or shops?

No. It teaches you how to judge fit and quality yourself, so you can shop anywhere with confidence.

The last word

You don't need to look younger.
You deserve to look like yourself at your best.
