Every spring, millions of families set billions of dollars on fire, and the money isn't for them. In Chinese tradition, the dead still have needs in the next world, so families burn hell bank notes at festivals like Qingming. You see, it's like mailing your grandfather a gift card, except the card is on fire. The notes come in values of millions and even billions, printed with the Bank of Hell and a very serious-looking ruler. Why billions? Because believers want their ancestors to live comfortably, not just get by. Luckily, paper costs almost nothing, so anyone can become a billionaire for a few cents. Unluckily, the afterlife seems to have terrible inflation, because every year the notes get bigger. Soon the money wasn't enough. Families began burning paper houses, paper cars, and even paper phones, in case grandpa wanted to upgrade. Nothing in this world can be spent there, yet people still pay. The billions go up in smoke, and the family walks away feeling richer.
