Tips for better videos

1. Title

Ek clear problem + curiosity. 70 characters se chhota rakho. “Ideas” button se 10 options milte hain.

2. Style

Documentary: facts, history, “why/how” topics. Animated explainer: simple concepts, kids, science. AI avatar: host wale channel (grandma, farmer) – host ki picture upload karo. Kinetic text: lists, shorts, fast facts.

3. Voice

“Listen” daba ke voice check karo. Hindi script ke liye hi-IN voice lo. Apni recording hai to “Upload my own voiceover” + wahi script paste karo.

4. Better B-roll

Owner panel me free Pexels aur Pixabay key daalo – tab real stock videos aate hain. Key ke bina AI images / free photos use hote hain.

5. Fix one scene, not the whole video

Video khol ke “Scenes” me kisi bhi scene ka text, search words ya picture badlo (apni image/video upload bhi kar sakte ho), phir “Save & render again”.

6. Test first

Pehle 30 second ya 1 minute + “Fast draft” se test karo. Pasand aaye to wahi title lamba banao.

7. Before upload

AI avatar / realistic AI visuals ho to YouTube ke “Altered or synthetic content” sawaal ka jawab YES do. Stock credits project page pe milte hain.
