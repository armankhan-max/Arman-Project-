#! A Last Word

You have made it to the end of the guide, which already makes you a more careful dresser than most men your age.

Good clothes are not about vanity. They are about walking into a room, a church, a restaurant or a grandchild's school play and feeling that you look like yourself at your best: not younger, not trendier, simply *put-together*. That feeling changes how you stand, how you greet people and how they greet you.

You do not need to do everything at once. Start with the trousers you wear most. Then the jacket. Then the shoes. Every fix you make will last for years and pay you back every time you get dressed.

Measure twice. Buy once. And let the tailor have the last word.

**Stay sharp, my friend.**
