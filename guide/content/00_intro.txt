#! Thank You for Buying This Guide

LEAD: You have probably stood in front of a mirror in a new jacket and thought, *something is off*, without being able to say what. This guide gives you the words, the checks and the fixes.

This is not a fashion book. There are no trends here and nothing you need to be young, slim or wealthy to use. It is a fitting-room manual: the same checks a careful tailor runs through in the first two minutes with a customer, written down so you can do them yourself at home, in a shop or with your tailor.

Most men over 60 were never taught how clothes should fit. Bodies change gradually: shoulders round a little, the waist moves, the neck changes, posture shifts. Meanwhile, shop clothes are cut for an "average" man who does not really exist. The gap between the two is why so many good men look slightly borrowed in their own clothes. That gap is almost always fixable, and usually for far less money than people expect.

### How to use this guide

- **In a hurry?** Do the *Quick Start* (page after the contents). Five minutes, one mirror, and you will know your three biggest problems.
- **Got a tailor visit coming up?** Go straight to Chapter 2 and the *Show This to Your Tailor* sheet at the back.
- **Buying for him?** Wives, daughters and sons: Chapter 10 and the size sheet are written for you.
- **Want the whole system?** Follow the 7-Day Action Plan near the end. One small task a day.

### Three promises

1. Every page gives you something to check, do, say or avoid.
2. Nothing here requires expensive clothes. Fit beats price every single time.
3. You do not need to change who you are. The goal is to look like the best-dressed version of yourself.

A short note: prices vary by town and country, so this guide talks about costs in relative terms only. Nothing in it is medical advice. If comfort is affected by a health condition, your doctor comes before your wardrobe.

Measure twice, buy once. Let us begin.
