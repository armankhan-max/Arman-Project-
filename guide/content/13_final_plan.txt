# [Final] Your 7-Day Action Plan

LEAD: One small task a day. By next week your wardrobe will be clearer, your best clothes will fit, and getting dressed will take two minutes.

| W=0.9,2.2,2.9 | Day | Task | Time needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The 5-minute mirror test | 5 minutes, plus 10 to note your three biggest problems |
| 2 | Clean out the trousers | About 30 minutes |
| 3 | Clean out jackets and coats | About 45 minutes |
| 4 | Shirts, knitwear and the chin test | About 45 minutes |
| 5 | Shoes, belts and accessories | About 40 minutes |
| 6 | The tailor visit | About an hour, including travel |
| 7 | Plan outfits and your shopping list | About 30 minutes |

## Day 1: See clearly

[ ] Do the Quick Start mirror test in daylight
[ ] Take front, side and back photos with your phone timer
[ ] Fill in the scorecard
[ ] Circle your three biggest problems and write them down
[ ] Measure yourself (or ask a family member) and start the size sheet in Chapter 10

## Day 2: Trousers

[ ] Lay out every pair of trousers you own
[ ] Run the five Keep/Fix/Remove questions (Chapter 4)
[ ] Try on every KEEP and FIX pair with your usual shoes
[ ] Mark the FIX pairs with a note: "hem", "waist", "taper"
[ ] Bag the REMOVE pile and put it by the door

## Day 3: Jackets and coats

[ ] Lay out all jackets, suits and coats
[ ] Do the shoulder check on every one. Wrong shoulders = REMOVE (unless sentimental)
[ ] Check sleeves, button and back for the ones that pass
[ ] Snip any tacking stitches still in vents or pockets
[ ] Empty every pocket
[ ] Move FIX items next to the trousers from Day 2

## Day 4: Shirts, knitwear and colour

[ ] Do the collar one-finger test on your shirts
[ ] Do the chin test (Chapter 3) with every shirt and sweater
[ ] Sort into flatters me / does not
[ ] Check knitwear for pilling, stretched cuffs and moth holes
[ ] De-pill what can be saved; fold all knitwear
[ ] Note which core colours you are missing

## Day 5: Shoes and accessories

[ ] Check heels and soles; set aside shoes for the cobbler
[ ] Polish every pair you are keeping
[ ] Put shoe trees in your two most-worn pairs
[ ] Check belts: match to shoes, replace cracked ones
[ ] Lay out ties and check length and width against your jacket lapels
[ ] Look at a photo of yourself in your glasses

## Day 6: The tailor

[ ] Fill in the *Show This to Your Tailor* sheet at the back
[ ] Take no more than three items, with the right shoes and shirt
[ ] Use the scripts in Chapter 2
[ ] Agree price and pickup date
[ ] Drop shoes at the cobbler on the way

## Day 7: Plan and list

[ ] Rehang the wardrobe by category, dark to light, with space between hangers
[ ] Compare what you own with the 20-piece capsule list
[ ] Write a shopping list of the gaps, in priority order (Chapter 5)
[ ] Lay out and photograph three outfits from Chapter 6
[ ] Donate the REMOVE bags

!TRY Put a reminder in your calendar for six months from today: "Wardrobe check". Repeat the mirror test, check the FIX pile, and update the size sheet.

# [Printable] The Fit Checklist

LEAD: Print this page or keep a photo of it on your phone. Use it in the fitting room and at home.

### Jacket

[ ] Shoulder seam ends at the shoulder bone; no dent at the top of the sleeve
[ ] Collar lies flat against the shirt collar at the back
[ ] Buttoned: front lies flat, no X-pull
[ ] I can reach forward and lift my arms comfortably
[ ] Sleeve stops at the wrist bone; a little shirt cuff shows
[ ] Length covers most of the seat; vents hang closed

### Trousers

[ ] Waistband at the natural waist
[ ] Comfortable sitting down; waist does not dig in or slide
[ ] Seat smooth: no pull lines, no pouch
[ ] Straight leg, not tight at the thigh
[ ] Hem just touches the shoe: slight break

### Shirt

[ ] One finger fits inside the buttoned collar
[ ] Shoulder seam at the shoulder bone
[ ] Stays tucked in when I raise my arms
[ ] Buttons lie flat, no gaps
[ ] Cuff reaches the base of the thumb

### Coat

[ ] Tried over a jacket or thick sweater
[ ] Shoulders just beyond my own
[ ] Sleeves cover the jacket cuff
[ ] Closes without strain; I can reach forward

### Sweater

[ ] Shoulder seam at or just past the shoulder
[ ] Hem reaches the belt
[ ] Skims the body, no clinging or ballooning
[ ] Cuffs and hem ribbing snug

### Colour and finish

[ ] Passes the chin test: face looks clearer and warmer
[ ] Goes with at least two things I already own
[ ] Belt matches shoes; socks match trousers
