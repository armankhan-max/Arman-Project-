# [Chapter 6] Outfit Formulas

LEAD: Thirty ready-made outfits, built almost entirely from the 20-piece capsule in Chapter 4. Find the occasion, follow the formula, and you are dressed in two minutes.

## How to read a formula

Each outfit lists the pieces from top to bottom, then a *finishing touch*: the small detail that lifts it. Swap colours freely using the combination chart in Chapter 3, as long as you keep to the same *level* of formality.

### The formality ladder

| W=0.7,1.5,3.8 | Level | Name | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Relaxed | Knitwear or casual shirt, chinos or jeans, casual shoes |
| 2 | Smart casual | Add a jacket *or* a collared shirt with a good sweater; leather shoes |
| 3 | Smart | Jacket and collared shirt, wool trousers, leather shoes; tie optional |
| 4 | Formal | Suit or jacket and trousers with a tie |
| 5 | Very formal | Dark suit, white or pale shirt, tie, polished black or dark shoes |

!TIP When you are unsure, dress one level up. Being slightly smarter than everyone else always reads as respect. Being the scruffiest person in the room reads as not caring, even when you do.

!TIP **The travel capsule:** navy jacket, two pairs of trousers (grey and stone), three shirts (two blue, one white), one navy sweater, one pair of comfortable smart shoes. Wear the bulkiest items on the journey. That is a week of outfits in a small suitcase.

## Weddings (Level 3–5)

Always check the invitation for a dress code. If it says nothing, Level 4 is safe.

| W=0.6,1.45,2.85,1.9 | # | Outfit | Pieces | Finishing touch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | Daytime wedding | Navy suit, pale blue shirt, a cheerful patterned tie, brown leather shoes | Pocket square in a colour from the tie |
| 20 | Afternoon garden wedding | Mid-grey suit, white shirt, sage or dusty pink tie, brown shoes | A buttonhole if offered |
| 21 | Evening / formal wedding | Charcoal or dark navy suit, white shirt, silver-grey or burgundy tie, black shoes | Shoes polished to a shine |
| 22 | Relaxed / beach wedding | Stone or light grey jacket, pale blue shirt, navy trousers, suede shoes, no tie | Sunglasses with a classic frame |
| 23 | Grandfather of the bride or groom | Your best navy or charcoal suit, soft white shirt, a tie in the wedding colours, polished shoes | Suit altered well ahead of the day |

!WARN Do not wear white or ivory as your main colour at a wedding, and avoid black unless the dress code requests it. And have your suit checked by a tailor **at least four weeks** before the day. Wedding season is the busiest time for alterations.
