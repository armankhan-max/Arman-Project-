## One-line rules to remember

### Fit

- Shoulders decide; the tailor does the rest.
- If the button pulls into an X, it is too small.
- Show a little shirt cuff: always.
- Trousers belong at the waist, not under it.
- The hem should kiss the shoe, not crash onto it.
- One finger in the collar.
- Fit the man you are today.

### Colour

- Navy is the new black.
- Soft colours near the face, deeper colours further away.
- Pale blue is your best shirt.
- One pattern is safe; two need different scales.
- Match socks to trousers and belts to shoes.

### Buying

- Buy fewer, better, and fitted.
- Never buy what you have to breathe in for.
- Price does not fit; tailoring does.
- If it matches nothing you own, it will hang unworn.
- Stop shopping when you are tired.

### Care

- Brush, rest and rotate.
- Fold knitwear; hang tailoring on wide wooden hangers.
- Cool water, no wringing, dry flat.
- Polish monthly; shoe trees always.
- Keep every spare button in one jar.

### Occasions

- When unsure, dress one level up.
- Weddings: no white, no black unless asked, tailor four weeks early.
- Funerals: dark suit, white shirt, dark tie, polished shoes.
- Travel: navy, grey and brown all match each other.

!NOTE If you remember only three rules: **shoulders first, show the cuff, hem to the shoe.** Those three alone fix most of what makes clothes look "off".
