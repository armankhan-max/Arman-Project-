## Description

Right now you can jump about half a meter. On the Moon, the same jump takes you 10 feet up. On
Pluto, it's higher than a house. On a tiny asteroid, you'd never come back down.

We take one normal jump to the Moon, Mars, Venus, the gas giants, icy moons, the Sun and a
neutron star, and find out what gravity really does to your body.

What world should we visit next? Tell us in the comments.
